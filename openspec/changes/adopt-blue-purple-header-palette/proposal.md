## Why

The application bar currently derives its background from the page surface, so it visually merges with the content beneath it and gives the forum no distinct brand surface. The product direction calls for a blue-purple branded header with white foreground text, while keeping the existing purple palette and adding restrained pink accents from the reference design. The header must stay verifiable against WCAG 2.2 AA, which the current surface-derived bar makes hard to reason about because its effective contrast changes with what scrolls behind it.

## What Changes

- Introduce explicit application-bar palette tokens: a blue-purple gradient (start, mid, end), a white on-bar foreground, and a white on-bar focus ring. The gradient lightest stop is chosen so white text remains at least 4.5:1.
- Make the top bar opaque and brand-colored instead of `color-mix`-translucent, removing contrast drift while content scrolls underneath.
- Restyle every top-bar child for the new surface: brand wordmark, desktop navigation links, compact menu button, account/logout link, identity label, pending label, language select, and focus rings.
- Replace the color-only current-page signal in the top bar with a redundant emphasis treatment (weight plus a visible underline indicator).
- Add a restrained pink accent token family for light surfaces only: section eyebrows, chip outlines, and notice borders. Pink is never used as the top-bar background or as small text on light surfaces.
- Adjust the skip link so it remains perceivable when it overlays the branded bar (light background with primary-colored text instead of primary-colored background).
- Update the browser `theme-color` metadata to match the new header.
- Keep the existing purple base palette (primary, containers, surfaces, outline, error) unchanged.

**Out of scope:**

- Community board logos, banners, thumbnails, or any administrator-uploaded board artwork. Board-level branding is deferred to a future change where community administrators upload their own images.
- Large decorative page-background stripe patterns; content areas stay visually quiet for reading.
- Dark mode, typography change, layout restructure, localization changes, and any behavioral change to navigation, dialogs, or forms.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

None.

### Modified Capabilities

- `accessible-material-interface`: the "Responsive Material presentation" requirement gains an explicit branded application-bar surface and a palette contract, including a contrast-verified scenario for the header foreground.

## Impact

- `src/style.css`: `:root` token block, `.top-bar` and all top-bar descendant rules, `.skip-link`, accent usage on `.notice`/`.chip`/section labels.
- `index.html`: `theme-color` meta value.
- `docs/accessibility-and-testing.md`: record the palette contrast evidence and the manual checks that automated scans cannot cover.
- `tests-browser/*.spec.js`: existing axe-based checks are expected to keep passing; no new route or interaction is introduced.
- `dist/` and `public/elm.js`: regenerated by the normal build; not hand-edited.
